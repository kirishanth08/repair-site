Our story

From one bench to six neighbourhood labs

Voltix started in 2012 as a single technician repairing phones out of a garage. Today we're a team of 18 certified techs across six labs — but we still run the shop the same way: transparent quotes, genuine parts and repairs we'd trust on our own devices.

Every device that passes through our benches is logged, photographed and tracked. When you drop off a phone, you get a repair ID that lets you follow each stage — from diagnosis to the final 30-point quality check.
